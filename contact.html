Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

🚗 Is parking available at the café?

Yes, dedicated valet parking is available right outside the café during all operational hours.

🐾 Is Aurelia Café pet-friendly?

Absolutely! Our outdoor veranda seating welcomes well-behaved pets, and we even offer complimentary puppy-cinos (whipped cream treat).

📶 Do you have Wi-Fi and power outlets for working?

Yes, we provide 300 Mbps fiber Wi-Fi and individual power sockets near all our wall booths.

🍰 Can I order whole cakes in advance?

Yes, our artisan cheesecakes and chocolate truffle cakes can be pre-ordered 24 hours in advance via WhatsApp Click-to-Chat.
